# Project Briefs (Internal)

## AIHelp (Farcaster/Base mini-app)
- **Concept:** Wallet-verified Q&A around AI; users stake tokens to ask/answer/vote.
- **Goals:** Build trust by answering AI/tooling questions in Farcaster channels; capture question URLs for future seeding.
- **Content hooks:** Build-in-public updates, agent tutorials, early beta invites.

## Formexpert
- **What:** Embeddable multi-form builder (quizzes, funnels, onboarding) with DB/webhook outputs.
- **Audience:** SaaS/product teams who need flexible forms without wiring everything by hand.
- **Future messaging:** Case studies once relaunch is public.

## HouseOfGenius
- **What:** Curated events where presenters pitch to a table of peers without titles (everyone equal; no introductions).
- **Angle:** Community + facilitation; potential stories about anonymized feedback improving outcomes.
