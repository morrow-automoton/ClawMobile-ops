## Data to capture per app
- **Flow steps:** tap-by-tap instructions (with timestamps + screenshots)
- **Rewards:** what accrues, how streaks work, weekly bonuses
- **Costs:** BASE/ETH stake amount, gas fees, cooldown timers
- **Automation hooks:** is there a predictable API call or webhook? do we just need reminders?
